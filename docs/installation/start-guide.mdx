Passionfruit is designed to help teams efficiently handle questionnaires, automate responses, and maintain consistency across all your compliance and reporting needs. Whether you're managing CDP reports, security questionnaires, or RFP responses, Passionfruit offers an intelligent platform that adapts to your team's workflow. This guide will walk you through the key features and help you get started quickly.

## Setting up Passionfruit for your organization

Now that you have an overview, let's get Passionfruit working for your organization.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up Single Sign-On">
    Let your team sign in with their work account. See
    [Single Sign-On (SSO)](/docs/installation/sso).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your documents">
    Sync the documents you already have from
    [SharePoint](/docs/sharepoint) or [Google Drive](/docs/google-drive).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your other systems">
    Connect your product data, ERP, or data platform with a
    [custom integration](/docs/custom-integrations).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Want to use Passionfruit inside Word, Excel, or customer portals? You can also install the [Microsoft Office Add-in](/docs/installation/install) and the [browser extension](/docs/installation/install#browser). This is optional.
